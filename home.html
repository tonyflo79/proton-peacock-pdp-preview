The whole build

AeroGrade Construction™

AeroGrade Construction is Proton's paddle build process, inspired by our founder's roots as an aerospace industry leader. Every paddle in the line starts the same way: aerospace grade carbon fiber as the hitting surface, with the rough texture structurally integrated into the carbon itself — roughness enhanced for optimized spin, not applied after. The face is rough because the carbon is rough. From there, each paddle takes on its own character through the high-performance core at its heart.

Charles Darling — Aerospace Executive, Proton Sports Founder

The founder behind the build

Charles Darling, an aerospace executive, designed AeroGrade Construction to deliver maximum performance and consistency. He brought the same advanced technology used in high-stress structural applications into paddle engineering: roughness built into the carbon itself, and cores designed for performance and longevity — a different core character for every style of play.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which paddle should I start with?+

Start with how you play. If you want control, durability, and the most forgiving sweet spot — the friendliest entry into the line — that's the Peacock and its Atomic Foam Core. If you play aggressive top-spin drops and want power with an ultra-responsive feel, the Flamingo's Velocity Hybrid Core is built for you. If you want touch and power in one elongated build, watch for the Roadrunner. Same aerospace face and spin across all three — the core is the choice.

What's actually different between the three paddles?+

The core. Every Proton paddle shares the same AeroGrade Construction — an aerospace grade carbon fiber face with the roughness structurally integrated for optimized spin. Inside, each paddle carries its own high-performance core: the Peacock's Atomic Foam Core for control and durability, the Flamingo's Velocity Hybrid Core for power and responsiveness, and the Roadrunner's Touch-Power Core pairing touch with power behind a carbon-Kevlar weave face.

Are Proton paddles legal for tournament play?+

Yes. Proton paddles are certified by UPA and USA Pickleball — the governing bodies that set the ceiling for sanctioned tournament play. Proton designs to the legal maximum of performance, so you get everything the rules allow, in any league or tournament that uses either spec.

How is the roughness different from a textured or coated paddle?+

The roughness is part of the carbon itself, structurally integrated during construction. It's structural — the face is rough because the carbon is rough. That's a different thing from a finish or treatment that sits on the surface. The texture is built into the aerospace grade carbon fiber, the same composite discipline used in high-stress structural applications.

Do Proton paddles come with a warranty?+

Series Three — Project Peacock carries a lifetime warranty on its construction: full coverage on the carbon face and the Atomic Foam Core for as long as the original owner plays. Warranty terms are specific to each paddle — see the paddle's page for exact coverage. Every order includes a 30-day money-back guarantee.
