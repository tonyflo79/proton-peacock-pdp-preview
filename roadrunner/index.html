FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will the Roadrunner be banned at my next tournament?+

No. Series Four — Project Roadrunner is dual-stamp certified by both UPA-A and USA Pickleball, the two governing bodies that set the ceiling for sanctioned tournament play — pushing the legal max. One paddle, both rule sets. It is playable at the legal ceiling of every league and tournament that uses either spec. If a future rule change moves the ceiling, we'll address it then.

How is the roughness different from a textured or coated paddle?+

The grit is part of the weave itself, engineered in during cure. The face is gritty because the carbon-Kevlar weave is gritty — not because of a finish or treatment sprayed on the surface. And because it's structural, it stays gritty across thousands of hits, where a coated texture wears smooth. The texture is built into the aerospace grade carbon fiber, the same composite discipline used in high-stress structural applications.

Why a carbon-Kevlar weave instead of plain carbon fiber?+

Most performance paddles use carbon fiber alone. The Roadrunner cross-weaves aerospace grade carbon with Kevlar on two axes — Kevlar is the same aramid fiber used where toughness matters most. The result is a face that grips the ball for spin and resists wear, so it stays gritty far longer than carbon alone. That's the durability half of "speed and durability."

What is the Touch-Power Hybrid Core?+

A hybrid of Nomex and polypropylene honeycomb, tuned together. Nomex brings firm, fast power; polypropylene brings softer touch and feel. Combined against the bi-directional weave, you get touch on the dink and power on the drive — one core, no compromise. The Roadrunner ships in a single dialed-in configuration: 16.5" Elongated, 15mm, royal blue.
